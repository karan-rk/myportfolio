Feature engineering

What the model hears

The pipeline does not use words or transcripts. It learns from measurable properties of the voice signal.

Zero-crossing rateChromaMFCCRMS energyMel spectrogram
These features describe changes in frequency, pitch content, energy, and spectral shape that can correlate with vocal emotion.

Model architecture

Why CNN + BiLSTM + Attention

Acoustic features→Conv1D→BiLSTM→Attention→8-class probabilities

Convolution layers identify local acoustic patterns, the bidirectional LSTM models sequence context, and attention emphasizes the most informative parts before classification.

Evaluation

How quality is measured

Five-fold validation tests whether performance generalizes across held-out samples. The project combines RAVDESS, CREMA-D, TESS, and SAVEE to increase speaker and recording diversity.

Accuracy is useful, but class-level precision, recall, and confusion patterns are the next evaluation artifacts to add as the project matures.

Product boundary

Designed for reflection, not diagnosis

The prediction is shown as a confidence-bearing signal inside a journaling workflow. It should help a user reflect on vocal patterns, not make medical claims.

A production deployment also needs authentication, encryption, retention controls, consent, monitoring, and careful evaluation across accents and demographic groups.
