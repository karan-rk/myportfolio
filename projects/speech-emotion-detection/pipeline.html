Engineering Decisions

Why CNN + BiLSTM + Attention

Acoustic features→Conv1D→BiLSTM→Attention→8-class probabilities

Convolution layers identify local acoustic patterns, the bidirectional LSTM models sequence context, and attention emphasizes the most informative parts before classification.

Reliability & Failure Handling

Validate before inference

FastAPI rejects unsupported, oversized, or unreadable audio before model execution. The product presents confidence and keeps predictions separate from medical claims.

Tradeoffs

Acoustic signal without transcript context

The model avoids transcript privacy and language dependence, but loses semantic context and remains sensitive to recording quality, accents, and dataset bias.

Future Improvements

Broaden evaluation and production controls

Add class-level metrics, calibration, demographic evaluation, authentication, encryption, retention controls, consent, and monitoring.

Angry: 86% — strongest class

High arousal with distinctive acoustic features (pitch, energy, rate) makes Angry the most separable class in the feature space.

Neutral ↔ Calm: most confused pair

13% of Neutral clips are predicted as Calm and 18% of Calm as Neutral. Both have flat prosody and low arousal, leaving few discriminating acoustic cues.

Fearful → Surprised: 17% bleed

Both emotions share elevated pitch and sudden onset. Without lexical context the model cannot always distinguish fear from surprise.

8-class vs 4-class difficulty

Published 4-class RAVDESS baselines typically reach 85–90%. Classifying all 8 classes — including the acoustically similar Neutral/Calm pair — is a materially harder task.
