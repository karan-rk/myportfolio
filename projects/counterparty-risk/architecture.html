Sentinel Decision-System Architecture

From longitudinal records to an explainable analyst action queue.

System objective

Rank future risk without hiding how a decision was produced.

The workflow separates feature validation, leakage-safe evaluation, calibration, explanation, and action delivery so model quality and analyst usefulness can be assessed independently.

Request / Data Flow

Records become a review queue

Validated monthly records move through temporal evaluation, champion-model calibration, local explanation, and risk-band assignment before reaching an analyst.

Engineering Decisions

Temporal validation over a random split

Using later months as the holdout better represents future production behavior and reduces the risk of information leaking backward in time.

Reliability & Failure Handling

Keep humans inside the decision boundary

Calibration checks, local explanations, monitoring outputs, and an inspectable queue make model drift or suspicious predictions visible before action.

Tradeoffs

Interpretability over marginal complexity

The measured logistic champion is easier to inspect and calibrate than more complex alternatives, at the cost of potentially missing nonlinear signals.

Future Improvements

Validate with real operational data

Add drift alerts, fairness analysis, threshold simulation, analyst feedback loops, and prospective evaluation before any real-world use.
